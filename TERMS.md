**Important physical safety notice:** Fitness Game Lab involves full-body movement in an immersive space, including punching, reaching, and swinging your arms, stepping sideways, crouching, and, in some games, jumping. Clear the area of people, pets, and objects. Respect Apple Vision Pro safety boundaries. Stop immediately if you experience pain, dizziness, nausea, fatigue, shortness of breath, or disorientation.

You agree to follow these safety requirements:

- Use Apple Vision Pro safely and follow Apple's instructions and warnings.
- Make sure Apple Vision Pro is fitted securely and comfortably before moving quickly.
- Do not strike people, walls, furniture, or other real objects.
- Make sure you have sufficient clear space in every direction, a flat, dry, non-slippery floor, and enough ceiling height.
- Do not play near stairs, windows, glass, or other hazards.
- Jump only if you can do so safely. If you are unsure, pause or stop the game instead.
- Take regular breaks and stay hydrated.
- Do not use the App while tired, impaired, or in a condition that reduces balance or coordination.
- Consult a physician before physical activity if you have a medical concern, injury, pregnancy, or physical limitation.
- Remain aware of your surroundings and stop if tracking is lost or behaves unexpectedly.
